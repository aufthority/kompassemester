About this app & your data

Kompas Semester is an independent study companion created by your lecturer for all chemistry courses — it is not an official KPJU system and does not hold your official grades or attendance records (those remain in INOVA). Signing up is optional.

What we collect: your full name, the email address you choose to sign up with, and your activity on this app — practice quiz results and study notes you write.

Why: to track your course progress, show you a study streak, and let your lecturer see engagement (not scores) for the course you enroll in.

Where it's stored: on Supabase, hosted in Australia (Sydney region). Your data leaves Malaysia to be processed there.

Who can see it: you, and the lecturer(s) of courses you're enrolled in. It is not shared with any other third party.

Your rights: you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting aufali@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your notes and quiz history from this app.
