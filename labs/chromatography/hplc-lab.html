Hints: four ideas
C18 (ODS) column: oily chains hold the non-polar part of a drug.
More methanol = stronger mobile phase = earlier peak.
Ion pair: hexanesulfonate (−) pairs with the charged drug (+) so it sticks to C18.
Peak area is proportional to amount. Compare the sample with a standard.
