1 · Purpose and scope

To confirm the identity of chlorpheniramine maleate in incoming raw material and in tablets, by comparing its IR spectrum with that of chlorphenamine maleate CRS.

2 · Specification

The spectrum of the sample shows absorption maxima at the same wavenumbers, and of similar relative size, as the spectrum of the reference prepared and recorded the same way.

Trainer’s note: judge the whole spectrum, especially the fingerprint region below 1500 cm−1. Matching groups above 1500 is not enough.

3 · Raw material: KBr disc

  1. Dry potassium bromide (KBr) before use.
  2. Grind 1–2 mg of the substance with 300–400 mg of dry KBr. Weigh the sample as 0.5% of the KBr weight.
  3. Press into a disc under vacuum.
  4. Prepare a disc of chlorphenamine maleate CRS in the same way.

Trainer’s note: KCl is used for hydrochlorides. Chlorpheniramine is a maleate, so KBr is fine.

4 · Tablets: extract first

  1. Weigh tablet powder equivalent to about 25 mg of chlorpheniramine maleate (one average tablet weight holds 4 mg).
  2. Disperse in 20 ml of dilute hydrochloric acid (1 in 100). Make alkaline (about pH 11) with sodium hydroxide solution.
  3. Extract with two 50 ml portions of hexane. Evaporate the hexane to dryness.
  4. Make a mull of the residue in mineral oil (Nujol). Treat 25 mg of the reference standard in the same way.

Trainer’s note: Nujol adds its own C–H bands near 2920, 2850, 1460 and 1377 cm−1. They appear in both spectra; ignore them.

5 · System and disc checks

Wavenumber scale: record polystyrene film. 1601.2 cm−1 and 1028.3 cm−1 must each read within ±1.0 cm−1.

Disc: reject a disc that is not evenly clear, or whose transmittance at about 2000 cm−1 (no band there) is below 60%.

Trainer’s note: water in the KBr shows as a broad band near 3400 cm−1 and another near 1640 cm−1.

Before you start: today’s procedure

The line at the bottom of the screen and the glowing box show your next move. STP, beside Hints, is the method you work from: it opens at the section you need now. Hints explains the ideas behind each step.

Hints: four ideas
Light atoms and strong bonds absorb at high wavenumbers.
Below 1500 cm⁻¹ is the fingerprint: unique to each compound.
A good KBr disc is dry and clear: at least 60% light through at 2000 cm⁻¹.
Identity: every band of the sample lines up with the reference.
