1 · Receiving reference standards

  1. Check the label: name, issuing body, batch, quantity. Read the leaflet or certificate for storage and the assigned value.
  2. Write the date received on the container and sign. Write the date opened when first opened. (GMP 6.21–6.22)
  3. Store exactly as the leaflet says. Log the standard in the reference standard register.
  4. The pharmacopoeial CRS is the primary standard; it is kept for qualifying the working standard (WS). Routine tests use the WS.
  5. A WS past its re-test date is quarantined and requalified against the CRS before use.

2 · Analytical balance

  1. Before use: level, clean, daily check passed.
  2. Repeatability: weigh a test weight 10 times; s = standard deviation. Minimum weight = 2000 × s (2 × s must be ≤ 0.10% of the amount weighed).
  3. Weigh only amounts at or above the minimum weight.
  4. Weigh by difference: vial before − vial after = mass transferred. Attach the printout.
  5. Let containers from the fridge reach room temperature before opening.

3 · Dissolution: standard solution

  1. Weigh accurately about 20 mg of chlorpheniramine maleate WS into a 100 ml volumetric flask.
  2. Add 30 ml medium to dissolve. Dilute to volume with medium and mix well.
  3. Dilute 4 ml of this solution to 100 ml with medium. Mix and filter.
  4. The final concentration is about 0.008 mg/ml (8 µg/ml).

Drug in the stock (mg/ml) = Std Wt × (P ÷ 100) ÷ 100, where P = % potency of the WS on an “as is” basis.

Trainer’s note: make up to volume with the bottom of the meniscus on the mark, eye level with it.

4 · Identification by TLC: chloroform

  1. Solution (1): extract tablet powder equivalent to 5 mg with chloroform, filter, evaporate to dryness with minimum heat, dissolve the residue in 1 ml chloroform.
  2. Solution (2): dissolve 50 mg of WS and dilute to 10 ml with chloroform.

Safety: read the chloroform SDS first. All open handling and evaporation in a fume hood. Gloves as SDS Section 8. Spills as Section 6. Waste to the halogenated solvent container.

5 · Records

  1. Write directly on the worksheet in permanent ink, at the time of the work. No loose notes.
  2. Correct a mistake with one line so the original stays readable. Write the right value, initials, date and the reason.
  3. No correction fluid, no overwriting, no pencil, no blank boxes. A second person checks and signs.

ALCOA+: Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available.

Before you start: today’s procedure

The line at the bottom of the screen and the glowing box show your next move. STP, beside Hints, is the method you work from: it opens at the section you need now. Hints explains the ideas behind each step.

Hints: four ideas
The CRS is primary. The WS gets its potency P by comparison with it.
Below the minimum weight, the balance error is too big. Weigh more.
Weigh by difference. Then correct for potency, then for each dilution.
One line through a mistake: the original stays readable.
